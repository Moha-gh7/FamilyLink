Features

Everything your family needs.

One app for tasks, rewards, chat, and more — no extra tools needed.

📋
Smart Task Management
Create tasks with deadlines, difficulty levels, and point values. Kids can request transfers or time extensions when life gets busy.
🏆
Points & Rewards
Every completed task earns points. Parents set custom rewards — ice cream trips, screen time, movie nights. Kids redeem when ready.
📸
Photo Proof
Kids upload a photo when done. Parents review and approve before points are awarded. No more "I already did it!"
💬
Family Chat
Quick messages between family members. Coordinate, celebrate, and stay connected throughout the day.
📊
Activity Feed
See who completed what and when. A live family timeline that celebrates every win and keeps everyone in the loop.
🎨
5 Themes
Violet, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, or Rose — each family member can pick a theme that feels like theirs.
How it works

Up and running in 3 minutes.

No tutorials needed. Anyone in the family can get started immediately.

1
Create your family
One parent signs up and receives a unique 6-character family code.
2
Invite everyone
Share the code. Each family member joins with their own account in seconds.
3
Assign & complete
Parents create tasks. Kids complete them, upload proof, and earn points.
4
Redeem & celebrate
Kids cash in their points for real rewards. Parents approve and fulfil them.
For every role

Built for parents and kids equally.

Different views, different powers — the right experience for everyone in the family.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Parents

Full control over the family. Create tasks, approve completions, manage rewards, and delegate responsibilities.

  • Create and assign tasks to any family member
  • Approve or reject completed tasks with photo proof
  • Create custom rewards and set point values
  • Approve reward redemptions and time extension requests
  • Delegate approval access to trusted family members
  • Change member roles and manage family settings
👦👧
Children

A simple, visual experience designed to feel fun and motivating — not like a chore app.

  • See their own tasks with clear urgency indicators
  • Start tasks and upload photo proof when done
  • Request time extensions with a reason
  • Transfer tasks to a sibling when needed
  • Earn points and track progress on the leaderboard
  • Redeem points for rewards parents have set up
